About Us

Kabila Yetu Ni Kenya is a movement built on one belief — that we are stronger as one people. We bring together leaders, youth, and communities to promote unity, shared identity, and purposeful action. Through mentorship, initiatives, and national conversations, we are shaping a generation that chooses peace and progress.
